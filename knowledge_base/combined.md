# Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication
Source: https://www.ie.edu/business-school/programs/masters/master-in-strategic-marketing-communication/
Category: standalone
Last fetched: 2026-05-17T15:48:45Z

## Overview
The Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication is a 10-month, full-time program designed to develop future marketing leaders at the intersection of strategy, creativity, and AI-powered decision-making. It provides a 360-degree exploration of the marketing and communication landscape, equipping students with skills in brand and reputation management, communication, and marketing. The program is for future-thinking professionals with a strategic business mindset who seek to lead organizations through strategic decisions and create business value.

## Format
Duration: 10 months, full-time. Modality: In-person. Language: English. Location: Madrid. Intake: September (2026-2027 intake starts September 7th). A part-time, blended Executive version is also available with an April intake.

## Curriculum
Common Core Period covering Strategic Foundations, Marketing Systems, Growth & Decision-Making, and Leadership & Transformation. Additional components include: Multimedia simulations and real-world challenges with companies like Google, Leo Burnett, and Diageo. Final consulting project generating real value for real companies. Optional certifications from digital leaders like Google and Salesforce. IE University Sustainability Certificate available. Optional international experiences including Global Immersion Week or Social Impact Week in London, Ghana, Amsterdam, or South Africa. Optional Career Specialization extension (three-month add-on for practical industry experience). Learning outcomes: Acquire 360-degree view of marketing and communication strategy; master strategic foundations, marketing systems, growth and decision-making, and leadership and transformation; develop skills in branding, communication, marketing and reputation management; learn to translate brand positioning into integrated strategies; apply knowledge through real-world projects; gain global perspective and professional network.

## Admissions
Requirements: Future-thinking professionals with strategic business mindset. A marketing/communications background is helpful but not required; cohorts include profiles from journalism, law, social sciences, humanities and other fields. Application steps and deadlines are not detailed on the provided page content.

## Overview
The Executive Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication is designed for future-thinking professionals with a strategic business mindset and at least three years of work experience who want to develop a 360-degree vision of marketing, communication, and digital strategies. The program shapes impactful leaders capable of driving business growth through branding, communication, marketing, and reputation management, preparing graduates for roles such as brand manager, communication director, crisis manager, and new media strategist.

## Format
Duration: 15 months. Modality: Part-time blended format combining online learning (asynchronous forums Monday-Thursday, live synchronous sessions on Saturdays) with three intensive face-to-face periods in Madrid (first and last weeks mandatory). Language: Not specified. Location: Madrid and Segovia. Intake dates: 2026-2027 intake starts April 13th.

## Curriculum
Four key pillars: Corporate Business & Management; Strategic Communication & Marketing; Communication & Marketing Plans; Tools & Skills for Communication & Marketing. Learning outcomes include: Marketing and communication strategies, brand positioning and multi-channel brand representation, branding and corporate identity, digital marketing and KPI measurement, stakeholder management, internal communication, and crisis communication. Final project: devise a solution to a real communications challenge faced by a real-world company.

## Admissions
Requirements: At least three years of professional work experience in marketing, communications, or related roles; strategic business mindset. Ideal candidate wants to upskill their career, expand knowledge in communication and marketing, and stay ahead of digital economy developments while continuing to work. ## Overview
This Dual Degree program combines IE Business School's International MBA with the Master in Public Policy from IE School of Politics, Economics & Global Affairs. Designed for ambitious professionals aspiring to work across public, private, and non-profit sectors, it equips graduates with advanced management skills and policy expertise to lead change at the intersection of business, government, and society. Graduates will be prepared to bridge corporate strategy with public interest and influence decision-making in complex, interconnected environments.

Dual Degree International MBA + Master in Public Policy


Dual Degree International MBA + Master in Public Policy

In today’s globalized and digital world, massive societal, political, and technological shifts are reshaping economies, governments, and industries. As traditional systems of governance and business models adapt, we need professionals who combine a deep understanding of policy with agile business acumen to lead change in an increasingly interconnected and complex world.

If you’re passionate about driving positive transformation at the intersection of business, government, and society, this Dual Degree program could be the perfect fit. It combines IE Business School’s world-renowned **International MBA** with the **Master in Public Policy** from IE School of Politics, Economics & Global Affairs. In less than two years, you’ll acquire advanced management and leadership skills while developing the policy expertise needed to shape global agendas and drive sustainable progress.

This program is designed for ambitious professionals who aspire to work across public, private, and non-profit sectors. By combining rigorous business training with a solid foundation in policymaking, governance, and technology for public good, you’ll be prepared to thrive in complex environments, influence decision-making, and contribute to solving the world’s most pressing challenges. Upon graduation, you’ll be equipped to lead responsibly—bridging the gap between corporate strategy and public interest.

**The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected.*
