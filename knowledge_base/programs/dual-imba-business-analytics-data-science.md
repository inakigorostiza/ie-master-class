## Overview
This Dual Degree program combines IE Business School's flagship full-time International MBA with the Master in Business Analytics and Data Science. It shapes students into innovative experts who leverage data science and advanced analytics to drive business value and innovation. The program is designed for professionals with prior career success who want to expand their capabilities in business, data, and technology to fulfill their professional potential.

## Format
Full-time; less than two years duration (varies depending on intake selected); taught in English; Madrid and Segovia campuses; multiple intake dates available

## Curriculum
The program pairs the International MBA curriculum with the Master in Business Analytics and Data Science curriculum. Students learn to identify, collect, analyze, interpret and use data to create value and drive innovation. The dual degree includes a unique six-month MXT (Management Xponential Technology) program focused on Management, Xponential Thinking, and Technology, which provides exposure to top tech leaders, CEOs, and founders for mentorship and exposure to exponential growth opportunities. Learning outcomes include developing business acumen, global perspective, and expertise in data science and advanced analytics to drive value and innovation.

## Admissions
Students should be ready to leverage the power of data and take their careers to the next level. For official degree requirements and specific prerequisites, see the registrar office requirements. Educational prerequisites must be provided and all requirements must be met on time to request the official university degree issued by the Spanish government.

## Career
Graduates are positioned to become dynamic and specialized professionals able to use data to drive value and innovation. The dual degree prepares students for leadership roles in the data-driven market. Alumni gain valuable skills in business, data, and technology applicable across industries requiring advanced analytics and business strategy expertise.

MXT: Management + Xponential Technology

MXT (Management Xponential Technology) is a unique six-month program designed for dual degree students from IE School of Science & Technology and IE Business School. Focused on Management, Xponential Thinking, and Technology, MXT equips ambitious students with the skills and mindset to leverage exponential growth and innovation opportunities in today's digital age, empowering them to lead with purpose and impact.

During the MXT, students will have the opportunity to learn, network, receive mentorship, and gain exposure from top Tech Leaders, CEOs, and Founders who will guide and inspire them to build and execute impactful solutions.
