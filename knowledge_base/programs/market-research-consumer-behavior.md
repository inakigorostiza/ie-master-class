## Overview
The Master in Market Research & Consumer Behavior is an innovative 10-month program designed to delve into neuromarketing and consumer psychology, uncovering motivations behind consumer behaviors. It integrates business, marketing, data analytics, and consumer behavior studies to enable deep insights through quantitative and qualitative research. Graduates develop informed business strategies and actionable plans to meet and anticipate market demands, preparing for specialized careers as market researchers, insights managers, data scientists, and behavioral analysts.

## Format
Duration: 10 months. Modality: In-person. Language: English (implied). Location: Madrid, Spain. Intake: September 7th, 2026 for 2026-2027 academic year. The program includes a Common Core Period with other marketing, communication and sales students, followed by specialized modules. Optional Global Immersion Week or Social Impact Week available, plus optional three-month Career Specialization extension.

## Curriculum
Common Core Period: foundational courses in business marketing & strategy, consumer insights & analytics, and consumer psychology & behavior, enhancing analytical, creative and digital capabilities.

Key areas covered: Consumer psychology and behavior, consumer insights, neuromarketing, business intelligence, AI-driven analytics, quantitative and qualitative market research methods (ethnography, surveys, analytics).

Electives: Third term offers choice between Data Analytics path or Consumer Behavior path.

Certifications: Optional Google, Salesforce, or IE University Sustainability Certificate.

Consulting Projects: Real-world challenges with companies like Nielsen, Millward Brown, and Telefónica.

International experiences: Global Immersion Week or Social Impact Week in locations such as London, Ghana, Amsterdam, and South Africa.

Learning outcomes: Decode consumer motivations through neuromarketing; develop expertise in consumer psychology and buyer behavior; craft business strategies as Consumer Insights Manager or Marketing Intelligence Strategist; apply research insights to real-world challenges; leverage insights for business growth.

## Admissions
Requirements: Recent graduates and early professionals seeking specialization in market research and consumer insights. Application steps and specific deadlines not detailed on provided page content.

## Career
Career opportunities include positions as qualitative researcher, ethnographer, insights and analytics manager, data scientist, behavioral scientist, product/business analytics expert, and market intelligence expert. Alumni secure roles in marketing intelligence, UX/consumer research, brand strategy, data analytics, and consulting. Top employers include Nielsen, Kantar, Ipsos, Johnson & Johnson, P&G, WPP, Telefónica, Amazon, Visa, Nike, Google, Microsoft, Apple, Diageo, and the Financial Times. IE University alumni network includes over 80,000 professionals across industries and borders, with 40 nationalities and 80% international students represented in class.

WHO IS IT FOR AND WHAT ROLES CAN IT LEAD TO?

The Master in Market Research & Consumer Behavior is perfect for recent graduates and early professionals who want to specialize in market research and consumer insights. Alumni land roles in marketing intelligence, UX/consumer research, brand strategy, data analytics and consulting at employers such as Nielsen, Kantar, Ipsos, Johnson & Johnson, P&G, WPP, Telefónica, Amazon, Visa and Nike.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM’S PILLARS AND DIFFERENTIATORS?

Five pillars frame the learning journey: Business Marketing & Strategy; Consumer Insights & Analytics; Consumer Psychology & Behavior; Professional Skills; and Consulting Projects. The mix emphasizes both qualitative and quantitative methods (e.g., ethnography, neuromarketing, surveys, analytics) plus real consulting projects for companies.

ARE THERE INTERNATIONAL EXPERIENCES OR ADD-ONS?

Yes. You can join the Global Immersion Week to network and gain international insights into career outcomes in international business markets or Social Impact Week to get first-hand experience developing MARCOM strategies while having a positive local impact. Recent destinations include London, Ghana, Amsterdam and South Africa, and past in-company visits have included organizations such as Google, Ipsos, Amazon, Diageo and the Financial Times.

After completing the core program, you can also add a three-month Career Specialization to refine your focus.
