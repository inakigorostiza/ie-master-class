## Overview
The Master in Creative Direction, Content & Branding is an intensive 10-month program that helps you bridge creativity and management to conceive, drive and implement creative projects from beginning to end. It equips you with tools to craft compelling content and visual projects that resonate with audiences, combining creative skills with strategic business acumen for roles in advertising, brand management, digital media and creative leadership.

## Format
Duration: 10 months full-time. Modality: In-person. Language: English. Location: Madrid, Spain. Intake date: 2026-2027 intake starts September 7th.

## Curriculum
Key areas include:
• Common Core Period with foundational courses shared with other Marketing, Communication & Sales programs
• Digital Media and Technology
• Visual Media and Creativity
• Business, Branding and Management

Learning outcomes:
• Harness creativity to drive growth through brand, storytelling, AI as a creative accelerator, content strategy and creative leadership
• Create content that resonates across platforms, audiences and formats
• Develop, manage and evaluate multi-format visual and digital media projects using AI as a creative tool
• Create transformational storytelling strategies in collaboration with industry firms
• Leverage storytelling power and produce innovative, compelling content aligned to brand communication needs

Optional add-ons: Industry certifications (Salesforce, AWS, Google), IE University Sustainability Certificate, Global Immersion Week or Social Impact Week with international trips, and optional Career Specialization (three-month extension for hands-on specialized industry experience).
