## Overview
This Dual Degree program combines the International MBA with the Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication, designed for professionals seeking to navigate the digital transformation of modern businesses. It equips graduates with creative marketing, management, leadership, and corporate communication skills to help organizations adapt to globalized digital challenges and build stakeholder relationships.

## Format
The duration varies depending on the intake selected. The program is delivered in a blended format combining the IMBA portion covering high-level international management, followed by the Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication focusing on communication and marketing strategy development. Located in Madrid and Segovia, Spain.

## Curriculum
IMBA portion: Covers basics of high-level management in an international setting
Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication portion: Develop strategies for communication and marketing plans, issue management, and corporate social responsibility (CSR)
Learning outcomes:
• Manage corporate reputations and communication with stakeholders
• Bridge the gap between businesses and consumers
• Develop effective business solutions and innovative communication initiatives
• Lead agile adaptation within organizations
• Combine managerial skills with corporate communication competencies

## Admissions
Applicants should be passionate about guiding organizations through the digital age with effective communication and innovative marketing strategies. Looking for a demanding international program that includes managerial skills and corporate communication competencies. For official degree requirements, students must fulfill requirements specified by the Registrar's Office to request the official University Degree. If educational prerequisites are not provided or requirements are not met on time, the student cannot request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government.

## Career
Graduates are prepared to confront pressing issues regarding communication and leadership in modern organizations. Career outcomes position graduates as versatile professionals with high-impact careers in communication. The program prepares professionals to work in corporate communication, marketing strategy, stakeholder management, and organizational leadership roles.

Dual Degree International MBA + Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication

The digital media revolution has created new challenges for corporations, which have in turn been forced to transform the way they operate. Modern companies must simultaneously adapt to the latest trends, publicize their brands and build closer relationships with their stakeholders. Since corporate communication is always evolving, professionals in this industry need to stay up to date with the latest developments and anticipate future changes. Today’s businesses are looking for people who can provide value with effective business solutions and innovative communication initiatives.

Through this cutting-edge Dual Degree program, you’ll gain the creative marketing, management and leadership skills needed to help companies navigate the new challenges posed by the globalized digital world. Over the course of the program, you’ll learn how to manage corporate reputations and communication with stakeholders, helping you bridge the gap between businesses and consumers.

During the IMBA portion of the program, you’ll cover the basics of high-level management in an international setting. Afterward, you’ll switch gears to the Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication, and learn to develop strategies for communication and marketing plans, issue management and CSR. Upon graduation, you’ll be fully prepared to confront the most pressing issues regarding communication and leadership, and lead agile adaptation within any organization. In other words, this Dual Degree will help you become the kind of flexible, versatile and highly competent professional that today’s corporate world demands.
