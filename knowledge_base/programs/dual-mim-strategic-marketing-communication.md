## Overview
The Dual Degree Master in Management + Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication combines organizational management with creative marketing to train students for 21st-century business leadership. This program prepares graduates to master new channels of communication, meet rigorous project management standards, and apply these skills in fast-paced corporate environments. It is designed for individuals committed to efficiency and transparency in corporate culture who aspire to become influential voices in the business world.

## Format

## Curriculum
The program combines two master's degrees: Master in Management (official degree) and Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication (university private degree). Students engage in classroom discussions, consulting projects, and personal development workshops to develop corporate management strategies and apply project management standards to marketing and communication roles.

Dual Degree Master in Management + Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication

Globalization has blurred boundaries and shifted perspectives to create a multifaceted global community of consumers, innovators and creators. The digital network that now connects the world encourages consumers to be more engaged than ever before. Companies are starting to work with them directly to design unique brands and develop effective management strategies. As a result, teamwork and international communication have a renewed importance in business. The Dual Degree in Management and Strategic Marketing & Communication trains students to master the 21st century’s new channels of communication.

This Dual Degree program combines organizational management with creative marketing. It trains students to be efficient and effective in business transactions, while also stimulating their creativity. The Master in Management teaches them to meet the most rigorous project management standards, while the Master in Strategic Marketing and Communication applies these new skills to fast-paced corporate environments.

Beyond the classroom, discussions, consulting projects and personal development workshops will help you nurture your own personal corporate management strategies. You’ll graduate ready to face the challenges of high-level positions in the corporate business world; design customer-friendly corporate policies; and secure your place in the future of business.

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE
