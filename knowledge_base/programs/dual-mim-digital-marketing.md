## Overview
This Dual Degree combines a Master in Management with a Master in Digital Marketing, designed for professionals who need both technical digital marketing skills and organizational leadership capabilities. The program equips students to interpret customer data, drive creative strategies, and optimize omnichannel customer engagement while developing the managerial expertise to lead organizational change in the digital age.

## Format
Duration varies depending on intake selected. Language and location not specified on page. Modality not specified on page.

## Admissions
The Master in Management is an official degree program. Students must fulfill requirements listed by the Registrar Office to request the Official University Degree upon completion. Educational prerequisites must be provided and requirements must be met on time; otherwise, the official degree cannot be requested as issued by the Spanish government. The Master in Digital Marketing is a University Private Degree from IE Universidad taught in IE Business School.

## Career
Designed to develop future leaders in digital marketing across industries. Graduates will be equipped to channel consumer voice in the digital age and transform data into actionable strategies for organizational impact.

Dual Degree Master in Management + Master in Digital Marketing

Digital disruption has transformed the advertising and marketing landscape, and forced marketing professionals to step up their game. It’s now more important than ever to listen to the customer, whose voice can be heard loud and clear across platforms, channels and media. Digital marketers must possess the analytical skills to interpret customer data, drive creative strategies and optimize customer engagement. Whether via smartphones, social media, video or email marketing (or all of the above), digital marketers have to create a seamless omnichannel experience that establishes a clear brand identity and sends a cohesive message.

With this Dual Degree, you’ll not only acquire the technical skills needed to understand consumer behavior—and the creativity to apply this information to new strategies—but will also develop the managerial know-how to drive positive change across an organization.

OFFICIAL DEGREE

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE


UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE

**The Master in Digital Marketing** is a University Private Degree from IE Universidad*, and taught in IE Business School. [ More information](https://www.ie.edu/official-and-private-degrees/) on what earning a University Private Degree from IE Universidad entails.
