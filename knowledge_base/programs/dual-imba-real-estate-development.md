## Overview
This intensive Dual Degree combines an International MBA with a Master in Real Estate Development, offering comprehensive foundation in the economic, financial, strategic and legal dimensions of real estate development globally. It is designed for individuals passionate about cities and the built environment who seek to become global professionals driving urban change, with expertise in management and real estate presented by industry experts.

## Format
The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected. Location: Madrid and Segovia. Language and specific modality (in-person/online/blended) not specified on page.

## Admissions
Students must fulfill requirements to request the issuance of the Official University Degree upon completion. If educational prerequisites are not provided and/or if requirements are not met on time, the student will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government. More information available at the Registrar Office.

## Career
Graduates become global professionals who drive urban change in the real estate development sector. The program prepares students to tackle emerging challenges in real estate development across all sectors.

## Contact
Senior Manager of Global Recruitment available for inquiries. Program specialist available for personalized advice on specific cases. Contact through 'WANT TO KNOW MORE?' section on program page.

Dual Degree International MBA + Master in Real Estate Development

Cities and real estate development play an important role in creating flourishing communities and bringing people and businesses together. As people continually flood to cities, real estate development can be seen as a major cause of booming urbanization. It’s important that social inequality and demographic trends are acknowledged in order to create sustainable cities. This coupled with the technological revolution—which has significantly impacted the professional real estate sector—has led to an increased need for creative professionals who are ready to tackle emerging challenges and embrace change.

This intensive Dual Degree will offer you a comprehensive foundation in the economic, financial, strategic and legal dimensions of real estate development all over the globe. You will receive a holistic look at international real estate, global competition and the development of cities. This specialized knowledge in real estate and expertise in management, presented by industry experts, will help you shine amongst your peers as you embark on your future career path and become an empowered leader in your chosen field.
