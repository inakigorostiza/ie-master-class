## Overview
The Dual Degree International MBA + Master in Finance combines IE Business School's internationally renowned IMBA with its specialized Master in Finance program. Designed for business leaders seeking in-depth expertise in financial markets alongside management and leadership skills, this program prepares professionals for senior roles in financial institutions, investment banks, hedge funds, and private equity firms. It provides complementary knowledge in general management and finance to help graduates stand out as versatile financial professionals capable of leading global organizations.

## Format
The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected. The program combines two master's degrees completed in less than two years. Location: IE Business School. Language and specific modality (in-person/online/blended) not specified on this page.

The program combines the International MBA curriculum with the Master in Finance curriculum. Specific modules, electives, and learning outcomes are not detailed on this page. OFFICIAL DEGREE


OFFICIAL DEGREE

**The International MBA is an official degree program.** Students must fulfill the following [requirements](https://www.ie.edu/registrar-office/) to be able to request the issuance of the Official University Degree upon completion of the Master Program. **If educational prerequisites are not provided and/or if the requirements are not met on time, the student will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government. In this case, the studies will not be official under Spanish educational regulations.**

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE


UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE

**The Master in Finance **is a University Private Degree from IE Universidad*, and taught in IE Business School. [ More information](https://www.ie.edu/official-and-private-degrees/) on what earning a University Private Degree from IE Universidad entails.

*IE Universidad is a University officially recognized by the Spanish education authorities, allowed to grant both University Official Degrees (Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD degrees), which are specifically regulated and accredited by Spanish Ministry of Education and University Private Degrees (Master), that are specifically designed by the University towards the professional application of their studies and the constant update of higher education, preserving the educational standards of excellence of IE. Both types of degrees are equally backed by the rigor and prestige of IE University.
