## Overview
This Dual Degree combines IE University's flagship International MBA with an innovative Master in Digital Strategy, AI & Innovation. Designed for professionals ready to become innovative leaders in the digital economy, the program equips students with expertise in digital technologies and artificial intelligence alongside rigorous management and business insight. In less than two years, graduates earn two degrees and gain a competitive combination of management, digital, and AI capabilities to drive innovation and transformation across all sectors.

## Curriculum
The Dual Degree combines the International MBA program with the Master in Digital Strategy, AI & Innovation. Students gain understanding of the latest digital tools, AI-driven strategies and technologies, along with management and business insight needed for practical application. OFFICIAL DEGREE

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE


UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE

**The Master in Digital Strategy, AI & Innovation **is a University Private Degree from IE Universidad*, and taught in IE Business School. [ More information](https://www.ie.edu/official-and-private-degrees/) on what earning a University Private Degree from IE Universidad entails.

*IE Universidad is a University officially recognized by the Spanish education authorities, allowed to grant both University Official Degrees (Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD degrees), which are specifically regulated and accredited by Spanish Ministry of Education and University Private Degrees (Master), that are specifically designed by the University towards the professional application of their studies and the constant update of higher education, preserving the educational standards of excellence of IE.
