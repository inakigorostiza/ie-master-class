## Overview
The Executive Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication is designed for future-thinking professionals with a strategic business mindset and at least three years of work experience who want to develop a 360-degree vision of marketing, communication, and digital strategies. The program shapes impactful leaders capable of driving business growth through branding, communication, marketing, and reputation management, preparing graduates for roles such as brand manager, communication director, crisis manager, and new media strategist.

## Format
Duration: 15 months. Modality: Part-time blended format combining online learning (asynchronous forums Monday-Thursday, live synchronous sessions on Saturdays) with three intensive face-to-face periods in Madrid (first and last weeks mandatory). Language: Not specified. Location: Madrid and Segovia. Intake dates: 2026-2027 intake starts April 13th.

## Curriculum
Four key pillars: Corporate Business & Management; Strategic Communication & Marketing; Communication & Marketing Plans; Tools & Skills for Communication & Marketing. Learning outcomes include: Marketing and communication strategies, brand positioning and multi-channel brand representation, branding and corporate identity, digital marketing and KPI measurement, stakeholder management, internal communication, and crisis communication. Final project: devise a solution to a real communications challenge faced by a real-world company.

## Admissions
Requirements: At least three years of professional work experience in marketing, communications, or related roles; strategic business mindset. Ideal candidate wants to upskill their career, expand knowledge in communication and marketing, and stay ahead of digital economy developments while continuing to work. WHO IS THE EXECUTIVE MASTER IN STRATEGIC MARKETING & COMMUNICATION FOR?

This program is best for future-thinking professionals with a strategic mindset and at least 3 years of work experience in marketing, communications or related roles. Cohorts include students who are driven by a desire to have a full understanding of communications, management and digital strategies. Graduates take on professional roles such as Brand Manager, Communication Director, Crisis & Reputation Manager, CSR/Social Impact Manager and New Media Strategist.

WHAT MAKES THE FORMAT EXECUTIVE-FRIENDLY?

The blended model of the Executive Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication combines weekly faculty-led asynchronous forums, live online sessions and three face-to-face periods in Madrid and Segovia. This lets you keep working while building skills and expanding your network through immersive workshops and classes.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM’S KEY LEARNING AREAS?

The Executive Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication has four pillars: Corporate Business & Management; Strategic Communication & Marketing; Communication & Marketing Plans; and Tools & Skills for Communication & Marketing.

WHAT TYPE OF DEGREE IS AWARDED AFTER THE EXECUTIVE MASTER IN STRATEGIC MARKETING & COMMUNICATION?

A University Private Degree from IE University, designed for professional application and continual upskilling. IE University is officially recognized by Spanish authorities, and its private master’s programs are designed for professional application and uphold IE University’s standards of excellence.

WHAT DOES THE PART-TIME, BLENDED FORMAT FOR THE EXECUTIVE MASTER IN STRATEGIC MARKETING & COMMUNICATION LOOK LIKE?

This part-time program allows you to upskill and expand your network without putting your career on hold. The unique blended format includes:

- Online periods: Get a challenging and highly interactive learning experience through our virtual campus, with well-structured, faculty-led sessions that fit into the modern professional’s busy schedule. Each lesson can be accessed anywhere with an internet connection.
- Live sessions: Connect to live video conference sessions every Saturday, engaging with your peers and professors from all over the globe in real time.
- Asynchronous online discussions: Participate in faculty-led asynchronous sessions from Monday to Thursday every week. Professors will moderate these sessions, covering chosen topics to achieve the learning objectives.
- Face-to-face periods: Take your learning to the real world and meet your peers in person through three intensive, face-to-face weeks in Madrid, with only the first and last weeks being mandatory.
