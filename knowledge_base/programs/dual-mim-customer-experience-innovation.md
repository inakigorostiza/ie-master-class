## Overview
IE University's Dual Degree in Management + Customer Experience and Innovation combines critical management skills with human-centered product and service design expertise. This program prepares students to become future leaders who can optimize online and offline experiences by strategically designing products and services centered around user and customer needs. Graduates gain the management knowledge and global perspective that today's companies seek, positioning them as innovative professionals ready to excel in the global marketplace.

## Format
The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected. The program is offered in a blended format with in-person components. Location: Madrid and Segovia. Language and specific intake dates are not specified on the page.

## Admissions
Application steps, specific requirements, and deadlines are not detailed on this page. For official degree requirements, students must fulfill requirements from the Registrar's Office. If educational prerequisites are not provided and requirements are not met on time, students will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government.

## Career
Graduates are positioned to become future leaders in customer experience and innovation across industries. The program prepares professionals to excel in roles where they can transform user and customer experiences and develop innovative products, services, and brands.

OFFICIAL DEGREE


OFFICIAL DEGREE

**The Master in Management is an official degree program.** Students must fulfill the following [requirements](https://www.ie.edu/registrar-office/) to be able to request the issuance of the Official University Degree upon completion of the Master Program. **If educational prerequisites are not provided and/or if the requirements are not met on time, the student will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government. In this case, the studies will not be official under Spanish educational regulations.**

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE


UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE

**The Master In Customer Experience & Innovation** is a University Private Degree from IE Universidad*, and taught in IE Business School. [ More information](https://www.ie.edu/official-and-private-degrees/) on what earning a University Private Degree from IE Universidad entails.

*IE Universidad is a University officially recognized by the Spanish education authorities, allowed to grant both University Official Degrees (Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD degrees), which are specifically regulated and accredited by Spanish Ministry of Education and University Private Degrees (Master), that are specifically designed by the University towards the professional application of their studies and the constant update of higher education, preserving the educational standards of excellence of IE. Both types of degrees are equally backed by the rigor and prestige of IE University.
