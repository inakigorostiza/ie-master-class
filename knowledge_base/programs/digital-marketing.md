# Master in Digital Marketing

## Overview
The Master in Digital Marketing is a full-time, in-person program designed to train future-ready digital marketers who can design omnichannel strategies, manage performance across platforms and apply data-driven decision-making from strategy to execution. It empowers you to become a proficient Digital Marketing Strategist that top companies are seeking, equipping you with deep understanding of digital tools, platforms and AI, essential for crafting personalized and effective marketing strategies.

## Format
Duration: 10 months full-time. Modality: In-person. Language: English. Location: Madrid, Spain. Intake: 2026-2027 intake starts on September 7th.

## Curriculum
Common Core Period: Foundational skills in creativity, analytics, sales, business strategy and digital marketing with peers from related disciplines.

Key Areas:
• Strategic Marketing
• Digital Marketing Tools
• Practical, Hands-on Labs

Core Learning Outcomes:
• Master digital platforms and channels, maximizing digital performance
• Design integrated omnichannel marketing strategies
• Become an expert in AI and digital campaign execution
• Develop expertise in AI and data-led marketing tech, e-commerce and CRM platforms
• Focus on performance analytics, major platforms and emerging trends in social media and influencer marketing

Optional Add-ons:
• Industry Certifications: Google Analytics IQ, HubSpot, BigML, Google and Salesforce certifications
• IE University Sustainability Certificate
• Global Immersion Week in hubs such as London, Ghana, Amsterdam and South Africa
• Social Impact Week
• Optional three-month Career Specialization for hands-on experience in specialized industry
• Capstone: Develop a marketing plan for real companies such as The Coca-Cola Company, IKEA, SHISEIDO and more

## Admissions
Requirements: Tailored to recent graduates and young professionals. Application steps and specific deadlines are not detailed on the page. Dual-degree options available with Master in Management or International MBA.

## Career
Career Outcomes: Typical roles after graduation include digital consultant, content strategy manager, social media strategist, SEO/SEM specialist, digital media manager, account executive, customer analytics specialist, Marketing Manager, Digital Marketing Analyst and Social Media Strategist.

Industries: Consumer Products & Retail, Banking/Consulting/Logistics, Marketing/Media/Technology, Healthcare/Pharma/Biotechnology, Fashion & Luxury, Education & NGO, Automotive & Sports.

Top Employers: Alumni work at companies including Google, Microsoft, Apple and major global corporations. The Coca-Cola Company, IKEA and SHISEIDO are cited as real-world project clients.

Alumni: 70% female, 95% international students, 14 nationalities represented in class. IE Business School alumni come from over 130 nationalities globally.
