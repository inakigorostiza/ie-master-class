## Overview
The Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication is a 10-month, full-time program designed to develop future marketing leaders at the intersection of strategy, creativity, and AI-powered decision-making. It provides a 360-degree exploration of the marketing and communication landscape, equipping students with skills in brand and reputation management, communication, and marketing. The program is for future-thinking professionals with a strategic business mindset who seek to lead organizations through strategic decisions and create business value.

## Format
Duration: 10 months, full-time. Modality: In-person. Language: English. Location: Madrid. Intake: September (2026-2027 intake starts September 7th). A part-time, blended Executive version is also available with an April intake.

## Curriculum
Common Core Period covering Strategic Foundations, Marketing Systems, Growth & Decision-Making, and Leadership & Transformation. Additional components include: Multimedia simulations and real-world challenges with companies like Google, Leo Burnett, and Diageo. Final consulting project generating real value for real companies. Optional certifications from digital leaders like Google and Salesforce. IE University Sustainability Certificate available. Optional international experiences including Global Immersion Week or Social Impact Week in London, Ghana, Amsterdam, or South Africa. Optional Career Specialization extension (three-month add-on for practical industry experience). Learning outcomes: Acquire 360-degree view of marketing and communication strategy; master strategic foundations, marketing systems, growth and decision-making, and leadership and transformation; develop skills in branding, communication, marketing and reputation management; learn to translate brand positioning into integrated strategies; apply knowledge through real-world projects; gain global perspective and professional network.

It is a University Private Degree from IE University (taught at IE Business School). IE University is officially recognized by Spanish authorities; private master’s programs are designed for professional application and uphold IE University’s standards of excellence.

IS THERE A PART-TIME OR EXECUTIVE VERSION?

Yes, there is a part-time version for working professionals. The Executive Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication is a part-time progam with a blended format and an April intake. It combines online periods with face-to-face weeks in Madrid.

CAN I COMBINE THE MASTER IN STRATEGIC MARKETING & COMMUNICATION IN A DUAL DEGREE?

Yes, there are Dual Degrees available. You can combine the Master in Strategic Marketing & Communication with the Master in Management or the International MBA.

WHAT IS CAMPUS LIFE LIKE IN MADRID?

You’ll study in Madrid, a major European capital with extensive public transport and a strong corporate presence (e.g., Google, Microsoft, Apple). Student life includes clubs, events and networking within the IE University ecosystem.
