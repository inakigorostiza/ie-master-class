## Overview
This Dual Degree combines IE University's world-renowned International MBA with a Master in Customer Experience and Innovation, preparing leaders to understand effective leadership strategies while developing specialized knowledge in emerging fields. The program is designed for individuals ready to enhance corporate leadership skills, deepen their knowledge, and revolutionize the customer experience through user-centered design and innovation. Graduates become corporate leaders capable of managing teams effectively while creating cutting-edge solutions for customers and buyers.

## Format
The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected. The program is completed in less than two years and is offered at IE Business School in Madrid.

## Curriculum
The program combines two degrees: the International MBA (official degree) and the Master in Customer Experience & Innovation (University Private Degree). Students gain a solid foundation in business fundamentals through the International MBA, then build upon this with specialized skills in customer experience design, user experience optimization, and product and service innovation. Learning outcomes include: developing a deep understanding of effective leadership strategies; mastering customer journey analysis; applying user-centered design principles; transforming customer experience and service design; thinking innovatively and applying learning in real-world situations; and designing personalized educational paths leveraging IE University's full resources.

## Admissions
Students must fulfill official degree requirements to request the issuance of the Official University Degree upon completion. If educational prerequisites are not provided and/or if requirements are not met on time, the student will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government. Contact Senior Manager of Global Recruitment Blanca del Cuvillos or schedule a personal consultation call with the Program Specialist for specific application steps and deadlines.

## Career
Graduates develop expertise in corporate leadership, customer experience optimization, and innovative product and service design. They are prepared to lead teams, drive innovation, and transform customer experiences across organizations. The program equips leaders to operate in today's digital world using user-centered design principles and modern business models.

UNIVERSITY PRIVATE DEGREE
