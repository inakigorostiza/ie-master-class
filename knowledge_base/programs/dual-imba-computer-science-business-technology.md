## Overview
This Dual Degree combines the International MBA with the Master in Computer Science and Business Technology, designed for tech-savvy professionals seeking to bridge technology and business leadership. Graduates develop unique expertise in both computer science and management, positioning them to lead technological innovation and drive business transformation across industries.

## Format
The duration of the dual degree varies depending on the intake selected. The program includes a six-month MXT (Management Xponential Technology) component designed for dual degree students. Language, location, and specific intake dates are not detailed on this page.

## Curriculum
The program combines two official degree tracks: International MBA covering marketing, strategy, corporate finance, and operations; Master in Computer Science and Business Technology focusing on latest tools, trends, and technologies. Graduates develop expertise in AI, cloud, cybersecurity, quantum technologies, and software architecture. The MXT component (six months) focuses on Management, Xponential Thinking, and Technology, providing networking, mentorship, and exposure to tech leaders, CEOs, and founders. Learning outcomes include fluency in computer science and management languages, ability to design and implement advanced technological solutions, and readiness for senior strategist and technology leadership roles.

## Admissions
Admission requires a background in Data Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technologies, or demonstrable professional experience in related fields. Individual assessment available if background is uncertain. Students must fulfill additional requirements to request the issuance of the Official University Degree upon completion. If educational prerequisites are not provided or requirements not met on time, the student cannot request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government.

## Career
Graduates are positioned to fill demand for well-rounded, highly skilled tech and business professionals. Career paths include senior strategists, technology leaders, digital transformation leads, tech-driven innovation directors, and global strategy managers capable of designing advanced solutions in AI, cloud, cybersecurity, quantum technologies, and software architecture.

MXT: Management + Xponential Technology

MXT (Management Xponential Technology) is a unique six-month program designed for dual degree students from IE School of Science & Technology and IE Business School.
