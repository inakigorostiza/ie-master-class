## Overview
This Dual Degree combines IE Business School's International MBA with IE School of Science & Technology's Master in Financial Technology (FinTech). It is designed for professionals seeking to bridge business strategy and financial technology, building both business leadership skills and cutting-edge technical expertise in the financial domain. Graduates emerge prepared to lead change in financial services, startups, consulting, regulation, or corporate finance.

## Format
Duration varies depending on intake selected. The International MBA component awards 60 ECTS credits. Specific modality (in-person/online/blended), language, location, and intake dates are not detailed on this page.

## Curriculum
The program combines the International MBA curriculum with the Master in Financial Technology curriculum. Students build business leadership skills and cutting-edge technical expertise in the financial domain, acquiring the ability to lead change in financial services, startups, consulting, regulation, or corporate finance. The program develops the mindset, tools, and networks necessary to shape the future of finance through creating digital platforms, innovating in payments, and designing financial systems. Specific modules, electives, and learning outcomes are not detailed on this page.

## Admissions
Students must meet enrollment conditions and requirements to be officially enrolled and entitled to request the official university degree upon completion. If educational prerequisites are not provided and/or if requirements are not met on time, the student will not be able to request the official university degree as issued by the Spanish government. Specific application steps and deadlines are not provided on this page.

## Career
The program prepares graduates for leadership roles in fintech startups, banks undergoing digital transformation, financial consultancies, and regulatory bodies. Graduates are equipped to innovate in finance, build digital products, solve complex regulatory and data-driven challenges, and drive impact in the financial industry.

In a world where business models are being transformed by data, AI, blockchain, and digital innovation — and where finance is not just about numbers but also about leveraging technology — there is an increasing demand for leaders who combine strategic management with deep technological fluency.
