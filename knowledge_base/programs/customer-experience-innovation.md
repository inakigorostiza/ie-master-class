## Overview
The Master in Customer Experience & Innovation empowers you to create impactful, human-centered designs in product, service, and experience sectors. By rethinking and redesigning the customer journey, you'll enhance consumer interactions and drive substantial value for businesses. The program transforms you into a leader in innovation through exploration of product innovation and management, service and experience design, business and growth strategy, and innovation skills. Graduates pursue roles such as strategic designer, innovation manager, CX specialist, UX designer, product manager, and chief experience officer.

## Format
10 months, full-time, in-person. Taught in English in Madrid. Intake in September; the 2026–2027 intake starts on September 7th.

## Curriculum
Common Core Period (foundational stage shared with other Master's programs) followed by tailored Master in Customer Experience & Innovation coursework. Four key areas: Product Innovation & Management, Service & Experience Design, Business & Growth Strategy, Innovation Skills. Practical learning through three Innolabs (Corporate Client Project in Term 1, Social Impact Project in Term 2, Sustainable Entrepreneurship Project in Term 3) where students deliver real-world solutions for corporate partners such as Microsoft, IKEA, and Santander. Optional three-month Career Specialization offering practical, real-world exposure within a focused industry sector. Optional additional certifications from Google, Salesforce, and IE University Sustainability Certificate. Immersive experiences include Global Immersion Week and Social Impact Week with visits to London, Amsterdam, Ghana, and South Africa. Learning outcomes include: designing impactful human-centered solutions; experience design, journey mapping, service redesign, and new value validation; creating seamless, synchronized experiences across all customer touchpoints; mastering leadership and design thinking; launching innovation in a global marketing ecosystem; developing insight generation, design thinking, prototyping, adaptive leadership, project management and human-centered innovation skills.

WHAT IS IE BUSINESS SCHOOL’S MASTER IN CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & INNOVATION?

The Master in Customer Experience & Innovation is a 10-month, full-time, in-person master’s that prepares you to design human centered, value driven products, services and end-to-end customer experiences by combining design, business and technology. The learning journey is organized around four pillars: Product Innovation & Management; Service & Experience Design; Business & Growth Strategy; and Innovation Skills.

WHERE IS THE MASTER IN CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & INNOVATION TAUGHT, IN WHAT LANGUAGE, AND WHEN DOES IT START?

The program is taught in English, in Madrid, full-time and in-person. Intake occurs in September.

WHAT PRACTICAL LEARNING CAN I EXPECT IN THE MASTER IN CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & INNOVATION?

Practical learning is at the core of the program. Most courses combine theory with hands-on individual and team-based work, allowing you to apply concepts to real business challenges from day one. This approach is most evident in the Innolabs, which are transversal, team-based labs that run throughout the three terms of the program. In these labs, you will work through the full creative process, developing skills in insight generation, design thinking, prototyping, adaptive leadership, project management and human-centered innovation. You will apply key concepts and frameworks to real challenges, strengthen your teamwork and consulting capabilities and deliver portfolio-ready, real-world solutions.

The three Innolabs are:

- Corporate Client Project (Term 1)
- Social Impact Project (Term 2)
- Sustainable Entrepreneurship Project (Term 3)

By the end of the program, you will graduate not only with knowledge, but with demonstrated experience creating impact across corporate, social and entrepreneurial contexts.
